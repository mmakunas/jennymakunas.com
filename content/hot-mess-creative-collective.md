A collective of musicians, DJs, producers, writers, multi-media artists, engineers, and friends, the Hot Mess world encompasses live and streaming performances, album releases, parties and events, and the occasional advertising undertaking. We’ve worked together - officially and unofficially - for over a decade, collaborating on work that brings us collective joy and brings our creative talents to the forefront.

We’re available for projects large and small! [Hit us up via email.](mailto:jenny@wearehotmess.com?subject=Who%20are%20Hot%20Mess?)

A pandemic-inspired weekly series of live streaming musical performances, DJ sets, and new music releases from Jenniflex and We are Galaxies, among others.

## Virtual Afterparties

A series of streaming afterparties following the weekly pandemic "Dinner and a Movie" event designed to raise money for causes such as ArtsFund, BlackVisionsMN, The Waterwheel Foundation, and The Divided Sky Foundation.

## Everything's Right  
&  
Screaming Through Space

Small series of parties at The Peacock Lounge in San Francisco featuring DJs spinning psychedelic house, disco, funk, and soul.

We believe dancing is good for your soul. It's easier to find music to dance to at 2am than it is at 8pm. That shouldn't be the case.
